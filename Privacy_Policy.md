# Privacy Policy for DTimestamp Overlay

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

## Data Collection and Usage
DTimestamp Overlay is designed with strict privacy in mind. This extension operates entirely locally on your device. 

* **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, analytics, or user data.
* **Local Storage Only:** The extension uses your browser's local storage solely to save your display preferences (such as selected timezone, timestamp format, and overlay position). This data never leaves your machine.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** Because no data is collected, absolutely no data is shared with, transferred to, or sold to third parties.
